Questions students actually ask

I'm not from IGDTUW or a CS background. Can I still come?

Yes — any college, year, and stream. Talks tagged Beginner friendly assume zero cloud knowledge.

Do I have to attend the whole day?

No. Check in once in the morning and attend whatever fits — you still get the certificate and recordings.

Is food included? What about allergies?

Lunch and snacks are free. Veg and Jain options always; allergen labels on every dish. Note severe allergies in the form and we'll coordinate with caterers.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes — an e-certificate within a week to everyone who checks in. No per-session tracking, no penalty for stepping out.

I registered but can't come anymore.

Email us so your seat goes to someone on the waitlist — no hard feelings.

Missed it, or want more?

The event shouldn't end when the auditorium empties.
